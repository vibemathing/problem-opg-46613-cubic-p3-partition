## Primary source and exact use

D. A. Holton, B. D. McKay, M. D. Plummer and C. Thomassen,
*A nine point theorem for 3-connected graphs*, Combinatorica 2 (1982), 53--62.
DOI: 10.1007/BF02579281.
Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02579281
Author bibliography: https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/publications

The publisher abstract states that any nine prescribed vertices of a 3-connected
cubic graph lie on a cycle. The author bibliography corroborates authors, venue,
year and pages. The publication is March 1982; 53--62 is the publisher's page
range. The narrower-looking article title must not lead to dropping cubicity.
The public preview contains the abstract and metadata, not the full proof.
No theorem number or full-proof audit is asserted. Access restrictions were not
circumvented, and no PDF copy was retrieved for this candidate.

Relation to the frozen root: **weaker**, a common-cycle existence statement for
nine frame vertices, not a universal P3-factor statement. Relation to the specific
C05 reuse gap: **exact** for the missing cycle through the nine retained vertices.
All retained vertices are distinct, and the frame is finite, simple, cubic and
3-vertex-connected. The separately supplied cycle-flow/gluing proof turns this
cycle into a P3-factor of the Petersen-replacement graph. No new axiom is added;
the published theorem remains an external proof dependency pending review.
